Step 1 · §1.1 / §4.1

Price → Driver: factor decomposition

Δs = βUSD·FUSD + βCarry·FCarry + βMom·FMom + ε. What is left, ε, is the object the narrative has to explain.

FUSD = equal-weight G10 basket vs USD (positive = USD weaker). FCarry / FMom = top-3 vs bottom-3 G10, inverse-vol weights, rebalanced monthly with legs frozen intra-month. Carry rank uses the short-rate differential PROXY (daily €STR / SONIA / EFFR, monthly OECD elsewhere); momentum rank is the 12-1 month return LIVE.

Betas (120D univariate) and the residual ε — last reading LIVE

Basket membership today

Validation episodes (§4.1): Apr-2025 should be FUSD-dominated, Aug-2024 a large negative FCarry

Failure mode: basket membership changes mid-month — legs are frozen within the month here.

Step 2 · §1.2

Macro — four differentials

Which economy is repricing faster, and through which channel? 60D momentum matters more than level. Each differential is A − B.

Growth: z of the 60D change across available growth series (IP, retail, payrolls, sentiment, local equities) — a true surprise index needs consensus data, not public. 1Y1Y real: US leg = 2·2y − 1y − 5y breakeven (daily); other legs = 10y nominal (monthly OECD) − CPI YoY, a weak proxy. Terms of trade: export(+)/import(−)-weighted commodity basket return. Fiscal impulse is typed from the IMF Fiscal Monitor / OECD; its sign is regime-dependent (positive under credibility, negative under fiscal dominance).

Terms-of-trade weights (export + / import −), editable
By constructionAnything the four differentials cannot explain is a regime or flow phenomenon → steps 4 and 6.
Step 3 · §1.3

Valuation — univariate short horizon, REER anchor

Δs = αk + βk Δxk + uk, one regression per driver. |z| ≥ 2 flags dislocation; value is an anchor, not a signal.

Regressions are on levels (log spot on the driver differential) over the rolling window; residual z = distance from the fitted relationship in units of the window's residual σ. Fits with R² < 0.05 or > 0.999 return blank (unmodelled regime / degenerate), never a dead fair value. LIVE equity diff · PROXY rate and inflation diffs where the foreign leg is a monthly OECD mirror.

Fade or follow — tiebreakers: breadth (pair-specific or bloc-wide?), options confirmation (is skew paying for continuation?), regime stability (step 6). Fade only when the residual is local, skew unmoved and axes stable.

REER stretch (BIS, monthly) — two uses only

Use 1: conviction haircut for positions pushing beyond ±2z (modest uplift for fades). Use 2: stop placement away from REER-consistent zones. It never triggers a trade. Source: BIS REER on FRED, 10y mean z.

Flows and positioning — the state variable

Event-risk overlay — next 4 weeks

Amplified → asymmetry widens; any position aligned with the crowd takes a mandatory size cut or a convex overlay. Meeting dates from the house calendar (status badge says confirmed / provisional / estimated).

Options measures (1M)

No public OTC FX surface exists. Type the desk's 1M ATM / 25Δ RR / 25Δ BF and the values 5 days ago for direction MANUAL; realised vol, E[RV] = 0.6·RV20 + 0.4·RV60 LIVE and the spot/RV correlation PROXY (stands in for spot/ATM-vol) come from public data. Synthetic surface generator: OFF.

Reading: RR level = insurance premium state; RR change diverging from spot = leading unwind tell; a flip of the spot/vol correlation sign (USD/JPY to "vol up when JPY up") marks a carry-unwind regime.

Step 5 · §1.5

Seasonality — two gates

Locate the window on 2000–2025 → Gate 1: |t| ≥ 2.25 on Apr-2021→now → Gate 2: same-sign significance in ≥ 3 of 4 fixed sub-periods → EV × λ (0.45).

Survivors (both gates) LIVE

All windows (audit record)

Score SS = dir·min(|t|/2.25, 1.5)·prox·(1−λ), prox = exp(−days-to-entry/10); zero unless both gates pass. EV net of a 2 bp round trip. Overlay on entry timing and size of trades that exist for other reasons, ≤ 10–20% of risk; standalone only with a written flow mechanism. Governance: realised vs expected hit rate is the decay alarm; retire fading windows; re-admit annually.

Step 6 · §1.6 / §4.1

Regime — five axes and one quadrant

Each axis −100…+100 = 100·tanh(z̄/2), reported as level and direction (20D change). Quadrant migration is the highest-value regime signal.

Direction and level together: "fragile and deteriorating" and "fragile but stabilising" are different trades. Components are z-scored on 2y PROXY; tanh hides extremes, so the raw z is shown below.

Raw component z-scores (last reading)

Quadrant — USD × real yield (10D centroid, ≥ 5-day persistence)

Step 7 · §2.1–2.5

Sub-strategies — lifecycle, carry basket, short vol, leg choice, trade card

Diagnosed, not felt: each state has a proxy signature and a playbook. One crash, two expressions.

2.1 Narrative lifecycle PROXY

Signature: ε moving against valuation + disagreement → Emerging; breadth ≥ 0.6, 5/20/60D consistent, |pos z| < 1.5 → Dominant; momentum steepening with RR paying up or new longs → Accelerating; crowded / RR extreme while spot stalls → Exhausting; quadrant migrating or persistent disagreement → Competing. Positioning and RR enter only when typed in step 4.

2.2 Long carry basket

w ∝ rank(c/σ)·(1−hcrowd)·(1−hskew), Σ|w| = gross from the gate. c = short-rate differential PROXY, σ = 1M IV if typed else 60D realised. Select on carry/vol, never raw carry. Funding-side risk (JPY/CHF positioning and their meetings) is first-class — see step 4.

2.3 Short FX volatility sleeve

VRP = IV1M − E[RV]. Core: delta-hedged 1M ATM straddles/strangles on the 3–5 widest-VRP pairs; mandatory tail buy-back 20–30% of premium into 10Δ wings; no net short vega in > 3 pairs sharing a funding currency. Carry and short vol share a single crash budget (§2.4).

2.5 Delta-one vs options — the FX leg

Question: can a linear position survive the catalyst, or does positioning turn it into a gap? Tells: lifecycle stage; VRP level and direction; crowding flag / event amplification; spot/vol correlation sign flip; CTA distance-to-trigger (Thematic toolkit).

Trade card — mandatory fields → scenario set → ½-Kelly × conviction

Checklist (0 = fails, 1 = passes) — weights 25/15/15/15/10/10/5/5

Scenario set (3–5), P&L in % of notional

Notional = ½f*·conviction × bankroll, capped by stomachable loss ÷ worst-scenario loss (the cap binds when tighter than Kelly). Scenario P&L is in % of notional, so f* is a leverage on the bankroll. Half-Kelly is the geometric-growth-consistent haircut for estimation error in p. A card failing ≥ 2 checklist blocks is flagged, sized at a reduced fraction and post-mortemed regardless of outcome.
